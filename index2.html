About Us

More Than Just Coffee

Home of the Bean is a cosy local coffee shop created for coffee lovers and the community. We serve freshly prepared coffee, homemade treats and tasty meals.

Whether you are meeting friends, studying, working or simply relaxing, our coffee shop is the perfect place to enjoy a warm cup of coffee.

"Good coffee makes every day better."

Why Visit Us?

  • Friendly service
  • Comfortable atmosphere
  • Fresh food and baked treats
  • Local South African business
